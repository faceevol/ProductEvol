FAQ

Before you create.

What is ProductEvol?

ProductEvol is an AI product-content studio for online sellers. Its seven tools cover photo enhancement, background changes, product scenes, ad imagery, social creative, product descriptions and short product video.

Who is the beta for?

Independent sellers, small brands and creators who need polished product content without a complicated stack of separate creative tools.

Will ProductEvol always preserve the exact product?

AI generation can sometimes alter visual details. ProductEvol should prioritize product fidelity, but every generated asset should be reviewed before publishing.

Can I use the output commercially?

Commercial-use wording should be synchronized with the final Terms of Service and the production model providers actually connected to the live service.

What does Beta mean?

The studio is usable while model selection, pricing, quality and workflows may still be refined based on reliability and user feedback.
